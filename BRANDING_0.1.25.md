# EliteInteliA Intelligence Factory — UI Branding

This build presents the product as an EliteInteliA Technologies product rather than C INVENT.

## Brand position
- Product name: **EliteInteliA Intelligence Factory**
- Parent brand: **EliteInteliA Technologies**
- UI positioning: **Enterprise Data & AI Intelligence Factory**

## UI direction
- Premium enterprise dark navigation with teal/cyan intelligence accents.
- White content surfaces and restrained borders for enterprise readability.
- Stage-based delivery language retained because it reflects the product workflow.
- Customer/project is presented as an **Engagement** in the primary navigation.
- Control plane is presented as the **Intelligence Control Plane**.
- Platform area is presented as **Platform & Consumption**.

## Brand guardrail
Technical Python package/module names remain unchanged (`c_invent`) to avoid breaking imports and persisted data. The user-facing product identity is EliteInteliA.
